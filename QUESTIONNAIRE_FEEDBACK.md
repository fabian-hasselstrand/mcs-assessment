# Questionnaire changes

The form collects operational and cost data for a site-specific business case and pricing proposal. Copy uses direct instructions and descriptions, without marketing headlines or slogans.

## Current revision

- Enlarged the desktop sidebar to 300–320 px, with larger navigation text and descriptions.
- **O02/O03:** current container position and ground layout are shown only where cleaning is already arranged. Sites without a current operation skip these questions. Removed the question about using the same position for robotic cleaning.
- **O06:** uses “cleaning chemicals” and asks for the percentage of containers requiring them. The percentage is included in the export. No chemicals means 0%; an unknown share stays unknown.
- **O08:** replaced “Other” with **OOG — out of gauge**, including optional details. Each container is counted in one category; shares still total 100%.
- **L01:** replaced “loaded cost” with **employer cost per person-hour**, defined as wages, employer contributions, benefits and shift premiums.
- **L02:** asks for elapsed time from the start of the cleaning cycle until the container is considered clean. This includes treatment or waiting within the cycle. It excludes pre-cycle queueing/positioning and post-cleaning handling/drying. Cycle time is not treated as staff working time.
- **L03:** replaced the total-turnaround estimate with an open question about the throughput bottleneck: cleaning, reach-stacker availability or handling speed, staffing, space, queues, contractor capacity, or something else. Exact timings are not required.
- **H01:** asks for allocated reach-stacker time and one combined machine-and-operator cost, per hour or per shift. Shift cost includes a shift-length field so it can be converted to an hourly rate. This question is limited to own-personnel cleaning of grounded containers. Contractor invoices remain the outsourced cost baseline.
- **F04:** removed the outgoing customer selling-price question. The core comparison uses operating costs. Additional services are assessed through approximate monthly container volumes.
- **V01/V02:** distinguish unmet demand (requests turned away or sent elsewhere) from containers eventually cleaned after a delay. Delayed containers already in the recorded volume are not counted as additional demand.
- **V03:** asks about recurring periods when cleaning is unavailable or interrupted, with operating days and estimated containers affected per year, plus causes and consequences. Includes partial-day interruptions.
- **V03A:** one open text field for additional costs and operational effects of cleaning delays and stoppages. The prompt suggests storage, yard space, handling, transport and overtime, with frequency and approximate costs if known. No structured cost breakdown or automatic calculation.
- **V04:** an approximate percentage of cleaned containers receiving claims or rejections, including cleaning-quality complaints. Count each affected container once. Previous monthly counts are not converted into percentages.
- **V05:** calls drying simply **Drying**. Drying, digital interior inspection and cleaning documentation each show one approximate monthly container estimate directly below the selected options. Removed V06–V08 and their pricing, timing, process and evidence questions. Existing monthly estimates carry over to V05; retired fields are excluded from exports. A container can use multiple services.
- **Water recycling:** remains an option with an optional field for arrangements or requirements, rather than a per-container service estimate.

Existing drafts retain their unchanged answers. Earlier “Other” percentages, active-cleaning times and separate handling costs are not silently assigned the new meanings. The form flags those entries for review. Removed fields and their dependent values are excluded from the review, submitted data and exports.

## Follow-up outside the form

1. Confirm the implementation schedule, site layout, utilities, operating responsibility and commercial terms before issuing a firm proposal.
2. Check completion time with one port director. The own-personnel route requires more detail than the contractor route and may need input from operations or finance.
3. Replace the chassis reference photo with a container in an actual chassis-mounted cleaning position when available. The supplied photo shows a container being lowered onto a chassis.
4. Confirm the demand and feasibility of additional services before including related revenue. Assess recycling costs, water quality and discharge requirements separately.
